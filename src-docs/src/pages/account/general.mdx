# General settings

Type `/settings` and open General. Each row is one switch. Enter edits the row you are on. These names are what the screen shows.

**Session name.** The name of this conversation. Same result as `/rename`.

**Workdir.** The folder this conversation is working in. Same idea as `/cd`.

**Awareness.** On, koma reads the project and keeps a short picture of it. Off, it skips that and does not spend a model call on it.

**Harness.** On, koma runs the extra safety check before it acts. Off, that check is skipped. Leave this on unless you have a reason to turn it off.

**Allowed dirs.** Other folders this conversation may use, beyond the one you started in. Same idea as `/adddir`. Ignored when Harness is off.

**Short-send.** On, koma sends a shorter packet of the conversation to the model. What you see on screen does not change.

**Bash shorts.** On, koma keeps a tidier log of commands it runs. On is the usual choice.

**Coding autosave.** This switch is for the desktop editor, not the terminal. Off means that editor saves only when you say so.

**Internet mode.** Simple or full. Same choice as `/internet`.

**Web search.** The search service. Built-in DuckDuckGo needs no key. Firecrawl, Tavily, and Exa ask for a key.

**Mouse capture.** Auto lets the wheel scroll the chat. Off leaves the wheel to your terminal, which helps when you want to drag-select text. On forces scroll capture.

**Max turns.** How many steps an extra assistant may take on one job. A higher number lets a helper keep going longer.

**Max out tokens.** How long a single reply is allowed to be. 0 means the usual large limit. Leave it alone unless a reply is being cut off, or you want shorter answers.

:::note[If this is not what you see]
The quick settings page shows Appearance and General together when the window is wide. Open General from that list if you do not see every row at once.
:::
